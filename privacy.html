Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Data Collection

Security Toolbox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All tools run entirely client-side in your browser. No cookies, no tracking scripts, no analytics — we do not have a server.

2. Third-Party Services

Some tools make HTTP requests to external services to provide functionality:

  • DNS Lookup — queries Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS (dns.cloudflare.com). Subject to Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • CVE Search — queries CIRCL CVE API (cve.circl.lu). Subject to CIRCL Privacy Policy.
  • HTTP Probe, Subdomain Scanner, SSL Checker, Port Scanner, Redirect Chain, CORS Checker — use allorigins.win as a CORS proxy. Subject to allorigins Privacy Policy.
  • GraphQL Introspection Checker — sends requests directly to the URL you provide.

The URLs you enter are sent to these services and may be logged by them. We have no control over their data practices.

3. Third-Party Advertising

This site does not currently display advertisements. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified.

4. Your Rights

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. For third-party services, refer to their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.

6. Contact

Open an issue on GitHub.
